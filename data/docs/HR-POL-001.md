# General Leave Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent employees of the Authority.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement
Employees are entitled to 30 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2.5 days per month of service.

## 3. Carry-forward of annual leave
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the following year, after which they lapse unless the line manager approved an extension in writing.

## 4. Requesting leave
Annual leave requests must be submitted through the HR self-service portal at least 10 working days in advance and approved by the line manager.
