# Supplier Invoice Payment Procedure

## 1. Payment terms
Approved supplier invoices are paid within 30 calendar days of receipt of a valid invoice and a matching goods receipt in SAP.

## 2. Checking invoice status
Employees can check the status of an invoice they raised by providing the invoice number and the purchase order number.
