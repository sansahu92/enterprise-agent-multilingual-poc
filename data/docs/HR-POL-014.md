# HR Salary Policy

## 1. Salary bands
Grade G7 positions are paid within salary band S4, from AED 42,500 to AED 51,000 per month. Placement within the band is decided by the HR Reward Team.

## 2. Annual salary review
Salary reviews take place each April. Increases above 6% require approval from the HR Director.
