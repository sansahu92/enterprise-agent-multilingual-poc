# Purchase Request Procedure

## 1. When a purchase request is needed
A purchase request is required for any purchase of goods or services above AED 5,000.

## 2. Required information
A purchase request must include a description of the goods or services, quantity, estimated cost, cost centre, preferred supplier if any, and a business justification.

## 3. Quotations
Purchases above AED 50,000 require at least three written quotations.
